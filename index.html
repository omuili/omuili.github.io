Research

Observation Reliability in Reinforcement Learning

I study how reinforcement learning agents can assess whether individual observations are reliable and how reliability information should influence sequential decision-making under observation corruption.

AI Evaluation and Robustness

I am interested in how evaluation design, distribution shift, adversarial transformations, and limited observations affect conclusions about the reliability and behavior of AI systems.

Interpretability and Model Behavior

I investigate methods for understanding model behavior, including internal representations, behavioral mechanisms, and causal interventions.

About

I work at the intersection of machine learning research, AI engineering, and scientific computing.

My broader research interest is reliable decision-making under imperfect information: understanding what AI systems can infer from the evidence available to them, when that evidence should be trusted, and how uncertainty should affect decisions.

I am also the founder of TraceVox, where I work on infrastructure for AI assurance, evaluation, observability, and reproducibility.
